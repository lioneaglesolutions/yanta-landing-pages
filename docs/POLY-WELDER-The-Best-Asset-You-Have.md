## Why I'd rank this above the diggers

You mentioned it last, almost as an afterthought. On the numbers it might be the strongest thing your parents own, for five reasons:

**1. It's genuinely scarce where you are.** The businesses renting 630mm-class butt welders in Australia — Acu-Tech Hire, Fusion Australia, Polyweld Machinery, Polywelder Hire — are based in Perth, Melbourne and Geelong. A Central Queensland contractor with a burst main or a pipeline job either waits on interstate freight or finds a local machine. **You're the local machine.** That's not a marketing angle, it's a structural advantage.

**2. Rental economics are far better than an excavator.** Low fuel, low wear, low hours, no operator seat time, and it fits on a truck instead of a float. Revenue per kilo of asset is dramatically higher than a 35-tonne excavator, and it doesn't depreciate the same way.

**3. The certification requirement forces the premium sale.** Poly fusion welding requires competency: **PMBWELD301E** for butt welding, **PMBWELD302E** for electrofusion, with biennial refresher assessment recommended. Most contractors ringing about a machine won't have a current ticketed welder. So the natural sale isn't the machine — it's **machine + certified welder + weld data reports**, which is worth multiples of a dry hire.

**4. It's the business your parents actually know.** Yanta's core work is poly welding, HDPE piping, tailings lines and plant installation. This isn't a diversification — it's renting out the thing they're already expert in. The credibility is real and it's already on the website.

**5. Search intent is perfect.** Nobody idly Googles "poly welder hire". Everyone who does has a pipeline job, a deadline, and a budget. Meanwhile the competition on those keywords in Queensland is close to nothing. See the Google Ads playbook — this is why poly welding gets its own campaign.

## The five offers, in order of value to you

### 1. ★ Machine + certified welder + weld reports — *lead with this*

> "We'll bring the Bushranger 630 and a PMBWELD-qualified welder, and you get weld records for every joint."

This is the offer. It bypasses the customer's competency problem entirely, it's the highest-margin option, and on pressure mains the **weld traceability** is often a contract requirement. Nobody buying a dry hire gets that.

### 2. Full poly welding subcontract package

They already do this — it's Yanta's core business. But it should be *marketed*, not just word-of-mouth. Anyone searching "poly welding contractor queensland" is looking for exactly this and is worth far more than a machine rental.

### 3. ★ The bundle nobody else can offer — *your real moat*

Look at what the fleet actually adds up to:

```
   14T EXCAVATOR + TRIMBLE GPS      →  trench dug to design grade, first pass
   +  TECNODUE BUSHRANGER 630        →  315-630mm fusion welds on site
   +  PMBWELD-CERTIFIED WELDER       →  compliant joints + weld data reports

   +  35T EXCAVATOR                  →  bulk excavation, backfill
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   =  A COMPLETE HDPE PIPELINE CREW FROM ONE YARD
```

**This is the single most valuable insight in everything I've built for you.** The fleet isn't a random collection of machines — it's a pipeline installation package, and the GPS matters *specifically* because pipe grades are exactly what machine control is best at.

No other supplier in Central Queensland can put trenching-to-grade, fusion welding, certified welders and materials handling on a site from one phone call. Contractors hate coordinating four suppliers. Sell the package.

> **"We'll dig it to grade, weld it, and hand you the weld reports. One supplier, one invoice."**

### 4. Emergency and shutdown response

A burst main or a failed line during a shutdown is a "right now, whatever it costs" problem. Local availability plus a certified welder plus a 630 machine is a genuine regional service gap. Charge a callout premium and don't apologise for it.

### 5. Dry hire to certified crews

Fine, and easy money — but **verify their tickets before the machine leaves the yard.** Ask for current PMBWELD301E/302E evidence and put it in the hire agreement. A machine handed to an unqualified operator making pressure-pipe welds is a liability you don't want.

I'm not going to invent numbers for a specialised machine. Get real ones this week:

- [ ] Price the **welder-supplied option** separately, as a day rate on top. Don't bundle it invisibly — make the customer see they're getting a qualified welder and weld records.

Bundling the consumable gear lifts the ticket and saves the customer three phone calls.

## What I need to know

Four things change how this gets executed, and I can't see them from here.

1. **Have you got a current PMBWELD301E/302E certified welder available?** The whole premium offer depends on it. Given Yanta's core business I'd assume yes, but the certification needs to be current, and biennial refresher assessment is the industry recommendation.
2. **What else is in the poly kit?** Electrofusion units, smaller butt welders (110–315mm), pipe cutters, re-rounding clamps, squeeze-offs, generators, pipe rollers, shelters. Each one is either extra hire revenue or a gap that sends the customer elsewhere.

You've told me 35T, 14T, 7T telehandler, utes and vans. Either the site's out of date or there's more gear than I've been planning around.
